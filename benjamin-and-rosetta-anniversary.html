Five chapters · One story

How we became us

My Rosetta, this is how I remember the beginning of our story—and why October will always mean so much to me.

Chapter One · Before 17 October 2023

The Feelings We Never Said

My Rosetta, before we ever became us, there was already something quietly beautiful happening between us. We were both admiring each other, carrying feelings in our hearts without revealing them. Two people who liked each other, yet neither had fully said what was there.

Sometimes I wonder what would have happened if we had both continued keeping those feelings a secret. Would our story ever have begun? Would we still be wondering what might have been?

Thankfully, our story did not remain a silent possibility.

Chapter Two · 17–22 October 2023

Kweikrom, Where Our Story Began

KWEIKROM CAMP MEETING · 17–22 OCTOBER 2023

The camp meeting at Kweikrom ran from 17 to 22 October 2023. Those days became part of the beginning of our story, leading up to the moment we officially chose each other.

To the world, it might have looked like an ordinary day. But today, I look back at it as the beginning of something that would become so precious to me.

We were already admiring each other, but neither of us had openly confessed our feelings.

Then you found the courage to ask for my contact.

My Rosetta, that little step means so much more to me now. You may have simply been asking for my number, but that moment helped open the door to a story we are still writing three years later.

I am grateful you found that courage. I am grateful our paths crossed. And I am grateful that the story of us did not end at that camp meeting.

✦ KWEIKROM CAMP · 17–22 OCTOBER 2023 ✦
Chapter Three · 27 October 2023

The Day That Changed Everything

After the camp meeting, we reached out to each other. The feelings that had once remained unspoken began finding their way into conversations.

And then came October 27, 2023.

That was the day we proposed to each other and officially began our relationship.

My love, that date became much more than a day on the calendar. It became the beginning of our journey, the day our silent admiration became something we could finally call ours.

Every October 27 reminds me that a beautiful story can begin with the smallest act of courage.

Three years later, I still treasure that beginning.

27 · 10 · 2023
THE DAY WE BECAME US.
Chapter Four · A season of distance

When Distance Tested Our Love

My Rosetta, our journey has not always allowed us to be as close as we wished.

At some point, school placed distance between us, and for more than a year, meeting each other was difficult.

There is a special kind of longing that comes when you care deeply about someone but cannot always be physically near them. Sometimes, the heart simply wishes for an ordinary moment together—to share a day, laugh together, or enjoy the comfort of being in the same place.

Distance became an unwanted part of our story. It reminded us that life does not always give us the moments we want exactly when we want them.

I will not pretend that distance is a beautiful thing. Sometimes, it is simply hard.

But when I look at our journey, I see more than the time we spent apart. I see the story that continued. I see the relationship that reached this third anniversary despite the difficulties of meeting.

My love, I hope our future brings us more opportunities to stand beside each other, create memories together, and enjoy the moments that distance once made difficult.

Our story is still being written, and there are so many chapters I hope we will get to experience together.

Chapter Five · 27 October 2026

Three Years Later, Here We Are

October 27, 2026.

Three years since we began. Three years since that Friday changed our story. Three years since two people who once kept their feelings hidden decided to start a journey together.

And here we are, my Rosetta.

When I look back at the beginning, I smile at how our story unfolded. When I think about the distance, I appreciate the moments we have been able to share. And when I look at today, I feel grateful that we have reached this beautiful milestone.

We have never fought since the day we started dating, and our journey has been amazing and wonderful to me.

Three years cannot tell the entire story of two people, but these three years will always be a special chapter in mine.

Today is not just a celebration of how long we have been together. It is a celebration of our beginning, the memories we have made, and the possibility of everything still ahead.

Happy third anniversary, my Rosetta.

The story that began in Kweikrom is still unfolding.
